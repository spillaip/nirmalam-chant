# Nirmalam Chant Privacy Policy

Effective date: 22 August 2026

Nirmalam Chant is designed to work without accounts, advertising, analytics, cloud synchronization, or third-party tracking.

## Information handled on your device

The app stores chant counts, timestamps, intentions, settings, and scheduled-practice details in local application storage on your device. This information is not transmitted by the app.

When you choose voice tracking, the app accesses the microphone solely to process live audio on the device and estimate chant counts. The app does not record, save, transmit, sell, or share microphone audio.

Notifications, if enabled, are local reminders scheduled by your device. The app does not send notifications through a server.

## Sharing and retention

Nirmalam Chant does not collect or share personal data with the developer, advertisers, analytics providers, or other third parties. Data remains on the device until you clear app data or uninstall the app.

## Your choices

You can deny or revoke microphone and notification permissions in Android settings. You can remove local app data through Android's app settings or by uninstalling the app.
