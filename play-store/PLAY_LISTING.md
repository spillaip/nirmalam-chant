## Full description

Nirmalam Chant is a calm, private space for mantra practice and meditation.

Keep your count with voice tracking or a manual tap. Set a personal target, follow mala milestones, save an intention, and review your own local rhythm over time.

Designed around privacy:

- No ads, trackers, analytics, accounts, or cloud sync.
- Microphone audio is processed only on your device and is never recorded or uploaded.
- Chant counts, intentions, and practice plans stay in local device storage.
- Optional haptic feedback, a gentle synthesized meditation tone, and local reminders.

Plan a practice, adjust the count for a particular session, and use Focus Practice for an uncluttered chanting screen.

Nirmalam Chant is a spiritual practice companion, not a medical device.
